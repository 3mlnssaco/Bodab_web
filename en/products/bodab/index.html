An app for older adults, built on UniQdata.io

Bodab

Bodab helps older adults manage medication, schedules and daily check-ins themselves, with large text and short steps.

Families can view records an older person has authorized for sharing in UniQdata.io's FamilyCare, within the scope of consent.

What can you do?

  • Large text and short instructions
  • Medication schedules and self-confirmation
  • Medical visit schedules
  • Daily check-ins
  • Choose what to share with family

Example use

Checking an older person's day

Older adults view their medication and medical visit schedules in the app and check in themselves. Families see only what the older person allows in FamilyCare.

Current scope

Bodab is a personal app for older adults. Institutional care requests and staff assignments are described separately in Bodab Senior.

INDIVIDUAL CONTROL · BLOCKCHAIN-BASED VERIFICATION

Sharing starts with individual choice.

Records made in Bodab connect with family within the scope the older adult allows. Family sharing and institutional care permissions are separate.

SportiQue’s sharing architecture separates personal record storage, consent and permissions, and blockchain-based evidence verification. Original health records and decryption keys stay outside the public ledger.

  1. Scope chosen by the individualRecipients, purposes and record scope are bound to consent.
  2. Delivery bound to permissionsThe design verifies recipient permissions and keys and connects encrypted delivery to the authorized scope.
  3. Evidence separate from originalsEvidence of consent, access and sharing is grouped into batches for blockchain verification, without publishing original records.
  4. Revocation and evidence reviewRevocation that blocks new access is separate from verification of existing history.
