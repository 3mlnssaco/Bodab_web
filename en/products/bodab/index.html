An app for older adults, built on UniQdata.io

Bodab

Bodab helps older adults manage medication, schedules and daily check-ins themselves, with large text and short steps.

Families can view records an older person has authorized for sharing in UniQdata.io's FamilyCare, within the scope of consent.

What can you do?

  • Large text and short instructions
  • Medication schedules and self-confirmation
  • Medical visit schedules
  • Daily check-ins
  • Choose what to share with family

Example use

Checking an older person's day

Older adults view their medication and medical visit schedules in the app and check in themselves. Families see only what the older person allows in FamilyCare.

Current scope

Bodab is a personal app for older adults. Institutional care requests and staff assignments are described separately in Bodab Senior.
