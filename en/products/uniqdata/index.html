Personal health management · Your record starts here

UniQdata

UniQdata.io is a personal health service for collecting scattered health records in a personal database and using them in daily life.

DataQ is an internal step that preserves originals and provenance while preparing record intake and standardization candidates. MediQ and MindQ are in-app experiences within UniQdata.io.

What can you do?

  • Health records at a glance
  • Daily logs and routines
  • Prepare questions before a medical visit
  • Review consent and access

MediQ: records before and after care · MindQ: mood and condition journal

Example use

Reviewing records after a checkup

Import checkup results, review originals alongside organized fields, and compare them with earlier records. Individuals decide the recipients and scope of any sharing with family or research.

Current scope

The starting point for personal health record management. DataQ is the internal step responsible for bringing records in and organizing them.

INDIVIDUAL CONTROL · BLOCKCHAIN-BASED VERIFICATION

Sharing starts with individual choice.

With UniQdata.io, individuals manage their records and choose which records to share and with whom.

SportiQue’s sharing architecture separates personal record storage, consent and permissions, and blockchain-based evidence verification. Original health records and decryption keys stay outside the public ledger.

  1. Scope chosen by the individualRecipients, purposes and record scope are bound to consent.
  2. Delivery bound to permissionsThe design verifies recipient permissions and keys and connects encrypted delivery to the authorized scope.
  3. Evidence separate from originalsEvidence of consent, access and sharing is grouped into batches for blockchain verification, without publishing original records.
  4. Revocation and evidence reviewRevocation that blocks new access is separate from verification of existing history.
