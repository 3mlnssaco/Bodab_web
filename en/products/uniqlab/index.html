Consented research operations

UniQLab

UniQLab.io is a workspace where researchers and institutions prepare studies and manage participants, consent and repeated data collection.

A working tool for research teams and institutions. It brings recurring documents, participant status and collaboration into one place.

What can you do?

  • Study preparation
  • Manage participant criteria
  • Review consent status
  • IRB document assistance
  • Institutional and researcher permissions
  • Data access history
  • Check visit schedules
  • Operations reports

Example use

A study with repeated check-ins

The research team defines the study period and collection fields, then runs check-ins within participants' consent. It reviews missing responses and provenance to organize study materials.

Current scope

An operations tool for research teams and institutions. Personal records are not automatically shared beyond the consented purpose and scope.
