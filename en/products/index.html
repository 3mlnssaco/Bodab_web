ONE NETWORK. DIFFERENT USES.

Start with your records.
Choose how to use them.

SportiQue is developing a personal health-data network where individuals collect, store, use and share their records under their own control, with visibility into permissions and use history.

FOR INDIVIDUALS

UniQdata.io

Bring scattered medical and daily health records together, understand them over time, and choose how they are used and shared. UniQdata.io is the starting point for the individual's personal record.

DataQ processes incoming records inside UniQdata.io, retaining their sources and helping normalize formats, fields and units.

Explore UniQdata.io ↗

FOR CONSENTED RESEARCH

UniQLab.io

A research workspace for study preparation, participant follow-up and record review. Individuals choose whether to participate and which records to share before their records connect to research workflows.

We are exploring connections to existing research systems so teams can work with additional participants and longitudinal records. Integration scope and participant availability need to be assessed for each project.

Explore UniQLab.io ↗

Care at home

Bodab supports medication check-ins, daily check-ins and schedules for older people and families. YAKSON is our medication-packet handling device project. These are distinct products with their own development and validation stages.
