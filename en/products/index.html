01 / Personal health management · Your record starts here

UniQdata

UniQdata.io is a personal health service for collecting scattered health records in a personal database and using them in daily life.

DataQ is an internal step that preserves originals and provenance while preparing record intake and standardization candidates. MediQ and MindQ are in-app experiences within UniQdata.io.

  • Health records at a glance
  • Daily logs and routines
  • Prepare questions before a medical visit
  • Review consent and access

MediQ: records before and after care · MindQ: mood and condition journal

02 / Consented research operations

UniQLab

UniQLab.io is a workspace where researchers and institutions prepare studies and manage participants, consent and repeated data collection.

A working tool for research teams and institutions. It brings recurring documents, participant status and collaboration into one place.

  • Study preparation
  • Manage participant criteria
  • Review consent status
  • IRB document assistance
  • Institutional and researcher permissions
  • Data access history
  • Check visit schedules
  • Operations reports

03 / An app for older adults, built on UniQdata.io

Bodab

Bodab helps older adults manage medication, schedules and daily check-ins themselves, with large text and short steps.

Families can view records an older person has authorized for sharing in UniQdata.io's FamilyCare, within the scope of consent.

  • Large text and short instructions
  • Medication schedules and self-confirmation
  • Medical visit schedules
  • Daily check-ins
  • Choose what to share with family
