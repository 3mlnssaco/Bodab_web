Medication assistance device PoC in development

YAKSON

YAKSON is a medication assistance device PoC being developed to record packet dispensing separately from the user's confirmation of taking medication.

It does not prescribe medication or automatically determine whether medication was taken. We are digitally reviewing the feeding and cutting of linked packets and the lower storage path for uncollected packets. The features below are development goals; final appearance and repeated physical testing are still pending.

View the current mechanism review ＋

A digital candidate reviewing the path for storing uncollected packets below and presenting one packet for the next scheduled dose. Final appearance, repeated physical operation and manufacturing suitability are not yet validated.

Features in development

  • Medication time reminders
  • Medication event records from buttons and sensors
  • Observed missed or delayed medication logs
  • Caregiver confirmation notifications
  • Check device connection and battery status
  • App connection
  • Usage reports
  • Home care testing

Example use

Reviewing the packet dispensing process

We are reviewing the flow that feeds, separates and presents one dose from linked medication packets at the scheduled time. The record of a packet being presented is separate from the user's confirmation of taking medication.

Current scope

A medication assistance device PoC. The images and video are digital reviews; repeated physical operation, medical-device classification and home deployment are not yet validated.
