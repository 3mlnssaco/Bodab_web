ABOUT

OUR REASON TO BUILD

A problem found through care.
Records were scattered.

While building Bodab, we wanted families to share an older person's medication, activity, medical visit and prescription records. Those records were scattered across hospitals, pharmacies and apps.
There were few services where individuals could bring those records together, manage them and choose which records to share with which family members.

SportiQue

THE INDIVIDUAL, FIRST

Control moves
from companies to individuals.

Health records are scattered across hospitals, devices, daily life and research. SportiQue is building a structure where records accumulate around the individual and can be used when needed.

Individuals decide with whom to share, for what purpose, to what extent and for how long. Research institutions are one of several uses, and the starting point is UniQdata.io.

01 / STORE

Your records, together.

Bring records from different sources together. DataQ preserves originals and provenance while standardizing formats, fields and units; UniQdata.io manages the records in the personal database.

02 / USE

Records for your daily life.

From reading and understanding records to preparing for care and daily routines, we connect the ways individuals use their own records.

03 / CHOOSE

Sharing decisions belong to you.

Individuals choose the recipient, purpose, scope and duration of sharing. We are building ways to review consent, withdrawal, access and use history.

OUR FOUNDATION / 2025

Putting record permissions
into practice through technology.

Our 2025 XRPL demo let users choose sharing scope and access duration, review access and use history, and receive research participation compensation automatically. Original health records remained in the personal database.
