OUR REASON TO BUILD

Health records centered
on the individual.

SportiQue is building a health-data network where individuals bring their records together, use them in daily life, care, AI and research, and choose where and how much to share.

SportiQue

THE INDIVIDUAL, FIRST

Control moves
from companies to individuals.

While building Bodab, we wanted families to share an older person's medication, activity, medical visit and prescription records. Those records were scattered across hospitals, pharmacies and apps.

There were few services where individuals could bring those records together, manage them and choose which records to share with which family members.

Health records are scattered across hospitals, devices, daily life and research. SportiQue is building a structure where records accumulate around the individual and can be used when needed.

Individuals decide with whom to share, for what purpose, to what extent and for how long. Research institutions are one of several uses, and the starting point is UniQdata.io.

WHAT WE BUILD TODAY

One foundation for records.
Many uses in life.

From personal record management to research and care, each product has a distinct role. Use and sharing begin with the individual’s choice.

01 / PERSONAL RECORDS

Personal health record management

UniQdata.io is the starting point for managing past medical and checkup records alongside today’s daily records. Within it, DataQ preserves originals and provenance while organizing formats, fields and units.

02 / CONSENTED RESEARCH

Consented records connected to research

UniQLab.io supports study preparation, participant consent, repeated record collection and review. Individuals choose participation and sharing scope in UniQdata.io; research teams work within that scope.

04 / FIELD IMPLEMENTATION

Record use adapted to the setting

We work with universities, research institutions and businesses to understand their existing materials and collection workflows, and prepare an approach suited to individual consent and their operating environment. Scope and integration feasibility are assessed through discussion and pilots.

We are preparing an approach to record organization and research operations pilots in Mongolia’s university and research settings. Actual operations and the scope of expansion remain to be tested in the pilot.

OUR DIRECTION

Across services and countries,
records stay with the individual.

Visit preparation, daily routines, family care and consented research. Even when the same records serve different purposes, we are building a network where individuals choose the recipient, purpose, scope and duration of access.

Our direction is for personal records generated in research to return to the individual, with each new use starting from new consent. Applications in each country are assessed against local policies and data storage requirements.

01 / STORE

Your records, together.

Bring records from different sources together. DataQ preserves originals and provenance while standardizing formats, fields and units; UniQdata.io manages the records in the personal database.

02 / USE

Records for your daily life.

From reading and understanding records to preparing for care and daily routines, we connect the ways individuals use their own records.

03 / CHOOSE

Sharing decisions belong to you.

Individuals choose the recipient, purpose, scope and duration of sharing. We are building ways to review consent, withdrawal, access and use history.

OUR FOUNDATION / 2025

Putting record permissions
into practice through technology.

Our 2025 XRPL demo let users choose sharing scope and access duration, review access and use history, and receive research participation compensation automatically. Original health records remained in the personal database.
