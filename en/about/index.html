THE INDIVIDUAL, FIRST

Control belongs
with the individual.

SportiQue is developing a personal health-data network where individuals collect, store, use and share their records under their own control, with visibility into permissions and use history.

It began with family care.

While building Bodab, we wanted families to share an older person's medication, activity, visit and prescription records. Those records were scattered across hospitals, pharmacies and apps. Individuals needed a way to bring them together and choose what to share with each family member.

Store

Bring records from different sources together. DataQ processes incoming records inside UniQdata.io while retaining originals and provenance.

Use

Understand your records and use them to prepare for care and support daily routines.

Choose

Decide the recipient, purpose, scope and duration of sharing. We are building ways to review consent, withdrawal, access and use history.

OUR FOUNDATION / 2025

Experience putting permissions into practice.

Our 2025 XRPL demo let users set sharing scope and access duration, review access and use history, and automate research-participation compensation. Raw health records stayed in the personal database. This is historical prototype work.

Founded by SeungHun Lee. Our current starting point is UniQdata.io. UniQLab.io is one research use individuals may choose.
