SPORTIQUE / GLOBAL VISION

Through personal records,
we connect the world.

A person's health records should continue across services and daily life, even across borders. Choices about storage, use and sharing remain with that person. SportiQue is building an individual-centered health-data network for this.

A visualization of the direction we are building toward. Availability in each country is assessed against local policies and data storage requirements.

MISSION / OUR ANSWER

Scattered records,
back to the individual.

To connect the world, we bring health records into the personal database and return decisions about use and sharing to the individual.

01 / STORE

Collect.

Manage records scattered across hospitals, devices, apps and daily life in a personal database, preserving originals and provenance.

02 / USE

Use.

Use records for the purposes you need: daily routines, visit preparation, care and questions for AI.

03 / CHOOSE

choose.

Individuals choose the recipient, purpose, scope and duration of sharing, and review consent and use history.

THE STARTING POINT / UNIQDATA

Records start
UniQdata.

UniQdata.io is the current starting point for managing personal health records. Within it, DataQ preserves provenance and originals while organizing formats, fields and units. Individuals use this foundation to choose how their records are used and shared.
